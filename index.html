Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about RISEONIC New Chandigarh.

What are the legal approvals for RISEONIC? ▼
RISEONIC holds 100% Site Registry®, approved CLU (Change of Land Use), Environment Clearance, Airport Authority Height Clearance, and fully approved Building Plans from the competent authorities.
What configurations are available? ▼
We offer palatial 3 BHK + Servant (3,284 – 3,729 Sq. Ft.) and 4 BHK + Servant (4,275 – 4,769 Sq. Ft.) dual-core residences with private sky terraces.
What makes Dual-Core apartments unique? ▼
Dual-Core apartments feature only 2 residences per floor elevator core, giving you private lift lobbies, zero shared walls for maximum noise insulation, and enhanced privacy.
Are bank home loans available? ▼
Yes, RISEONIC is pre-approved for home loans with leading nationalized and private financial institutions including HDFC Bank, SBI, ICICI Bank, and Axis Bank.
