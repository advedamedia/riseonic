About the Project

Where Ambition
Meets the Sky

RISEONIC stands alone as New Chandigarh's tallest building at G+35 floors. Positioned on Stadium Road (PR-4) with a commanding 318 ft frontage, this north-east facing landmark spans 6.58 acres of prime land.

Dual-core architecture ensures every residence is a corner home, engineered for panoramic views, optimal Vastu orientation, and unmatched natural ventilation. Double basement parking infrastructure delivers seamless convenience for every resident.

Questions

RISEONIC is New Chandigarh's tallest building at G+35 floors, located on Stadium Road (PR-4), Mullanpur, New Chandigarh.

RISEONIC offers ultra-luxury dual-core apartments in two configurations: 3BHK + Servant (3350 sq.ft.) and 4BHK + Servant (4275 sq.ft.).

RISEONIC is a 100% site-registry approved project. Contact sales team for exact RERA registration numbers and official project approvals.

In a dual-core design, the building has two separate service cores (lifts and lobbies), giving each home corner-unit privileges, extra light, and privacy.
