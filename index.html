RISEONIC is New Chandigarh's tallest building at G+35 floors, located on Stadium Road (PR-4), Mullanpur, New Chandigarh. It is the TriCity's first-ever project to offer Terrace Homes — a pioneering residential concept.

RISEONIC offers ultra-luxury dual-core apartments in two primary configurations: 3 BHK + 1 (3284 - 3792 sq.ft.) and 4 BHK + 1 (4275 - 4769 sq.ft.) layouts, designed for premium spacing and luxury comfort.

Pricing for RISEONIC apartments is available on request. Please contact our sales team or fill out any enquiry form to receive the detailed floor-wise pricing, current inventory availability, and payment plans.

RISEONIC rises to G+35 floors — Ground plus 35 upper levels — making it the tallest residential building in New Chandigarh (Mullanpur). Its 318 ft grand frontage and dual-core design further set it apart from any other project in the TriCity.

RISEONIC is a G+35 approved development and a 100% site-registry approved project. Construction is progressing rapidly in full compliance with RERA Punjab schedules. You can contact our sales team to schedule a physical tour of the construction site.

RISEONIC is fully registry-approved. For RERA registration number and official documentation, please contact our team. We will provide all legal documents for verification.

In a dual-core design, the building has two separate service cores (lifts, staircases, lobbies), which means fewer units per floor and more privacy for every resident. It also results in better ventilation, corner-unit advantages, and superior natural light for each apartment.
